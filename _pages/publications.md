My research experience spans motion transfer, adaptive visual representations, and sign language recognition.

<h2 id="samnet">SAMNet: Semantic-Aware Motion Retargeting via Canonical Motion Space</h2>
**Undergraduate thesis · December 2025–June 2026**

**Chinese title:** 基于规范运动空间的语义感知运动重定向网络<br>
**Author:** Zihan Wang (王子寒)

- Developed a unified deep-learning framework for motion retargeting across human skeletons with different joint counts, topologies, and body proportions.
- Introduced semantic-aware skeletal conditioning and a dual-path architecture combining cross-topology attention with a topology-normalized canonical motion space.
- Studied unsupervised zero-shot retargeting to previously unseen skeletons, with improved motion accuracy over existing methods as reported in my CV.

This is thesis research; no publication venue or accepted paper is listed here.

<h2 id="adaptive-dilated-convolution">An Adaptive Adjustment Method for Dilated Convolution</h2>
**First inventor · August 2023–April 2024**

**Chinese patent number:** ZL 2024 1 0530741.4

- Developed an adaptive dilated convolution network that selects dilation rates through an HSIC-based mutual-information loss.
- Implemented a confidence-based selection module with SoftMax-weighted dilation kernels.
- Reported a 3% accuracy gain on VOC2007, leading to a published patent.

<h2 id="sign-language">LSTM-Based Sign Language Recognition and Voice Translation</h2>
**Independent project · October 2022–June 2023**

Built a real-time sign language recognition system using Orbbec depth cameras and an LSTM sequence model, with depth-aware constraints for motion robustness and background filtering.

**Recognition:** Best User Experience and Outstanding Spatial Solution, Orbbec & NVIDIA 3D Vision Competition.
