<h2 id="qwen3-math">Domain-Specific Fine-Tuning of Qwen3-8B for Math Tasks</h2>
**Competition project · October 2024–July 2025**

Fine-tuned Qwen3-8B using **LoRA and MindSpeed-LLM** on curated Modelers datasets. The project achieved a **2.5× accuracy gain** and **120% throughput improvement**, as reported in my CV.

**Award:** Cloud Star Award, China Telecom AI Yunxirang Cup Competition (**top 1%**).

## NestOrder: A Message AI Assistant
